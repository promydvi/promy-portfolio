Methodology

My Localization Workflow

A rigorous, multi-phase translation and quality control pipeline designed to guarantee absolute native accuracy.

01

Analysis & Setup

Reviewing source files, setting up term bases, establishing custom glossaries, and configuring CAT tool environments for consistent styling.

02

Translation & Adaptation

Drafting the translation while adapting cultural nuances, idioms, and technical terms to match the target audience's expectations.

03

QA & Verification

Executing automated checks using Xbench, cross-verifying tag placement, checking source alignment, and eliminating grammatical bugs.

04

Delivery & Support

Exporting final files in correct formats (SRT, VTT, HTML, DOCX), checking visual layout, and providing responsive post-delivery support.
